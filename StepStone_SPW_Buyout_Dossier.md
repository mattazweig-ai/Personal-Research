## 3. The exact buyout formula (Option Agreement §7)
**Gross Purchase Price (§7(p))** = `[ (2 × Adjusted Adviser ANI, trailing 6mo) + (60% Total Current-Yr Incentive Fees + 40% Prior-Yr annual-fund + 100% Prior-Yr quarterly-fund) − tax@STEP blended rate (22.6%) ] × Applicable Sale% × Purchase Multiple`

Earnings cascade:
- **Basic ANI (§7(f))** = net mgmt & advisory fees + incentive fees + realized investment income + interest income + other income − realized perf-fee comp − recurring opex − interest expense − other loss.
- **Adviser ANI (§7(d))** = Basic ANI − fees on SSG balance-sheet investments in the Adviser's products − Legacy HNW Feeder Mark-ups + Actual Distribution Costs − Amortized Distribution Costs.
- **Adjusted Adviser ANI (§7(c))** = Adviser ANI − Incentive Fees + perf-fee comp. *(Incentive fees re-enter via the 60/40/100 line — not double-counted, but the buyout base DOES capture SPRING performance fees.)*

**Purchase Multiple (§7(hh))** = lesser of 20x and (Percentage Factor × SSG Multiple). **SSG Multiple** = 10-day VWAP ÷ LTM ANI (StepStone's own P/ANI). **Percentage Factor** = 70% if SSG ≥ 20x. → **14.7x** at 3/31/26 (= 70% × ~21x).
**Net Purchase Price (§7(bb))** = Gross − returned seed ("Reference Date Net Investment Amount" = $18,638,153) − Net Investment Amount + cash + capex − distributions ± Earnout/Earndown (capped 10%; Additional Earnout up to +5%).
**Consideration (§4(f))** = cash default; up to 75% in SSG units (10-day VWAP), 30% free / 23.3/23.4/23.3% locked yrs 1–3.
**Put/Call:** Put Only Apr-1-2026→Jun-30-2027 (first put ≥80%, Put Cutback to 80%); Put/Call from Jul-1-2027 (100%); Call no earlier than Sept-30-2027.
*(Schedules A–G — incl. the Basic-ANI worked example and the Peer Set — were referenced but NEVER filed.)*

## 4. The key finding — performance-peak ceiling, not run-rate
Back-into the disclosed range at 14.7x and 22.6% tax:
| Bound | Implied pre-tax base | Composition |
|---|---:|---|
| Max $2,257.6M | **~$198M** | includes SPRING incentive fees |
| Min $564.4M | **~$50M** | ≈ recurring management-fee earnings |

Profit-share "amount" attributable to PI: **$3.1M (FY24) → $23.2M (FY25) → $136.2M (FY26)**. FY26 decomposition: **~$53M recurring FRE + ~$83M one-quarter SPRING incentive-fee crystallization** (Q3 FY26: $14.4M FRE + $83.2M performance). Firm incentive fees +582% to $220M FY26 (SPRING/CY2025-return driven; SpaceX fed the NAV but was not a realization). Management guided incentive fees to **moderate**. The formula's 2×-6-month annualization **amplifies** a hot quarter.

**SPW fee-economics cross-check** (from fund prospectuses): SPRIM 1.40% · SPRING 1.50% + 15% incentive · STRUCTURE 1.60% · STPEX 1.60% · SCRED 1.15% + 10% · blended ~1.36% on ~$13.2B US registered AUM. Recurring FRE ≈ $57–100M (35–50% margin), **2–4× short of the $198M ceiling** — and a ~50/50 sub-advisory split could halve StepStone-retained revenue. → **The ~$2.26B is propped up by elevated, variable performance fees; normalized base maps toward the ~$564M floor.**

**On "51%" / profit-share %:** CH's economic % of SPW is **NOT disclosed** (only in the unfiled Adviser LLC Agreement); SPW standalone ANI is **NOT disclosed**. "51%" ≈ $136.2M ÷ $264.6M after-tax ANI (51.5%) — an *artifact*, not an ownership stake. (Quarterly PI ÷ ANI even hit 146% in Q3 FY26 — structurally impossible for an ownership share.)

## 7. The new $3.1B Collateralized Fund Obligation (CFO/CFE)
- "Structured Solutions Vehicle for Private Market Secondaries," closed **Mar 31, 2026**; $3.1B commitments ("largest of its kind").
- Notes outstanding $931.2M = **$736.9M senior secured (7.59%, bullet April 2041)** + $194.3M subordinated; up to **$1,396.8M** more capacity; **$408.4M Alter Domus liquidity line** (SOFR+270, due Mar-2031).
- **Ares** (primary capital) · **Barings Portfolio Finance** ("anchored the rated debt") · **Citi** (structuring/placement). **Privately rated; Cayman SPV; issuer name not public.** Non-recourse.
- Consolidated because StepStone is the VIE **primary beneficiary** (power as collateral manager + retained ~half the equity) — same logic as **Carlyle/AlpInvest's** consolidated CFO. *No connection to the SPW buyout — that linkage would be our thesis.*

## 8. Consolidation rule (why the CFO is on-balance-sheet)
- **VIE — ASC 810-10-15-14:** thin equity / equity holders lacking power → VIE (so the "majority equity" voting rule doesn't apply).
- **Primary beneficiary — ASC 810-10-25-38A:** consolidate if you have **both** "the power to direct the activities that most significantly impact economic performance" **and** "the obligation to absorb losses or right to receive benefits that could potentially be significant." **No ownership-% threshold**; "only one reporting entity… will have the power."
- **CFE measurement — ASU 2014-13 (ASC 810-10-30-10→15, 35-6→8):** measure the CFE's assets & liabilities using "the more observable of" the two.
- Peers: **Carlyle (AlpInvest)** consolidates a CFO on the same basis; Blackstone/Apollo/KKR/Ares/Blue Owl/Hamilton Lane do **not** currently consolidate a fund-interest CFO (their "CFE" mentions are CLOs).
