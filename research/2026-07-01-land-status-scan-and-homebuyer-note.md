arena site and asked for a full scan of filings/news/records on the land status.

## Confirmed current reality
- Land is **OPTIONED, not bought.** Arena is a **2031 target**, pre-entitlement: no zoning
  approval, no disclosed financing mix, no renderings, no published cost.
- Sellers: Seritage (~20 ac, $50.76M), Beck Ventures (majority — separate deal still needed),
  Life Time Inc. Buyer vehicle: Arena Development Intermediate, LLC.
- **Arena-independent activity IS real:** *Premier at Dallas Midtown* (296 units + 13.5k sf
  retail) under construction, opening ~2027 — district redevelops somewhat regardless.
- **Market backdrop:** Dallas home values **~$315k, down ~4.6% YoY** (Zillow). **No data**
  shows arena-driven appreciation in the immediate blocks; bullish claims = broker marketing.
- Opposition exists (South Dallas leaders / CM Maxie Johnson: "investment moving north").
- Vision-stage only (low confidence): Live Nation 4–5k-seat venue, 4-star hotel, training complex.

## Homebuyer guidance (as RE partner — not financial advice)
1. **Underwrite the house as if the arena never comes.** Schools/condition/commute/resale on
   its own merits. Treat arena upside as a *free option you don't pay up for.*
2. **Don't pay an "arena premium."** Sellers/agents cite the June 1 announcement; the arena is
   contingent (option, unfunded, un-zoned, 2031, can die in DD). Paying up = paying for a maybe.
3. **Proximity is double-edged.** Walking-distance = land-value upside BUT event-night traffic/
   parking/noise (egress is the weak point; mitigation unfunded — see traffic memo). **~1–2 mi
   out may capture halo appreciation without the game-night misery.** Where exactly matters.
4. **Peak uncertainty right now** = less bidding competition but more risk. Buying is fine;
   paying up for the arena is not. Re-check after the ~Aug 10-Q, a zoning filing, or financing news.
