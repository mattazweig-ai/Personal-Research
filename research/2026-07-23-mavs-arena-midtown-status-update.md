# Mavs Arena + Valley View / Dallas Midtown — Status Update (as of July 23, 2026)

Focused on what actually moves the needle for a nearby homebuyer: **is the arena deal real
and proceeding, and what concrete development is coming to the area.** Civic/PID items
(district HQ, social-services centers) excluded as not decision-relevant.

## VERDICT: The arena deal is proceeding. High confidence. (No termination.)

The June 30 walk-away date passed and — instead of backing out — the buyer *escalated
commitment* in July. Two hard, dated signals:

### 1. Legends Global hired as owner's rep (~July 21–22, 2026) — strongest signal
- Mavs named **Legends Global** (the Jerry Jones / NY Yankees-backed firm) as **owner's
  representative + premium sales partner**, "from pre-design through closeout" —
  budgeting, construction schedule, contractor/architect liaison.
- You do not hire a construction owner's rep and a premium-seat sales partner for a project
  you're about to abandon. This is behavioral confirmation they **stayed in the option**
  past June 30, even though the formal filing confirmation won't land until the 10-Q.
- Sources: D Magazine (Jul 2026), CelebrityAccess (Jul 21), The Real Deal (Jul 22).

### 2. Mark Cuban went to court (July 9, 2026) — confirms a live, high-stakes deal
- Cuban (sold majority in 2023 but **retains ~27%**) filed a **Texas Rule 202 pre-suit
  petition** in Dallas County to compel majority owners (Patrick **Dumont** / Adelson family)
  to disclose the **arena purchase contract, financing, corporate structure, and related
  development opportunities.** Alleges he was shut out ("adversarial business practices").
- Read-through: (a) the deal is real and consequential enough to litigate over; (b) the
  **financing/structure is genuinely opaque** — a 27% owner is having to sue to see it, so
  it's not hiding in any public doc. Also introduces some ownership/governance friction.
- Sources: The Real Deal, NBC DFW, Yahoo/AP (all Jul 9, 2026).

## The area is being built regardless of the arena — this is the durable story

**Beck Ventures "Dallas Midtown"** (the master plan wrapping the arena parcel):
- Scope: **~$4–5B, ~10-year rollout** on the ~110-acre Valley View site — ~1.5M sf retail,
  **~4,000 residential units**, office towers, hotel/condo tower, green space.
- **Phase 1 — "The Premier at Dallas Midtown" is real and moving dirt now:** $85M, **296
  luxury apartments + ~13.5K sf retail** at Preston Rd & Dilbeck Ln. Developer Anthem +
  Beck, with **equity from PLT America (Toyota + Panasonic JV)**. Grading done; vertical
  underway; **opens 2027, residents early 2028.**
- This proceeds **independent of whether the arena closes** — so the neighborhood's upgrade
  has real, funded momentum on its own. The arena would be an accelerant on top.

**City posture:** Dallas City Council/staff have signaled they'll move quickly on the
zoning/entitlements. A formal arena **entitlement/rezoning application to the City Plan
Commission has not yet surfaced** — that will be the next public, on-the-ground milestone.

## Home-value read (from DFW real-estate commentary)
- Well-planned arena + mixed-use districts tend to lift values **within ~2 miles**; the main
  downside risk is **traffic/congestion/parking** if infrastructure lags. Nearest residential
  corridors: Far North Dallas / Preston Hollow (75240, 75254, 75230) and the Galleria area.
- For a buyer: the **cheap-option structure + the still-opaque financing + a 2031 arena
  target** all argue against paying an "arena premium" today. The Beck development is the
  more reliable near-term value driver.

## What to watch next (the only things that raise confidence further)
1. **Seritage Q2 10-Q (~mid-Aug 2026)** — slated to attach the full option PSA and state
   post–June-30 status. Near-certain formal confirmation.
2. **Cuban's Rule 202 proceeding** — could force financing/structure details into the open.
3. **First City of Dallas zoning/entitlement filing for the arena** — earliest hard sign the
   Mavs are executing on the ground.
