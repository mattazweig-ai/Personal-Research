## THE LOCATION — the single most important variable
- The **Valley View / Dallas Midtown district is ~440 acres** bounded by the Dallas North
  Tollway, LBJ (I-635), Preston Rd, and **Alpha Road**.
- **Encore Dr sits off Preston near Alpha — the district's NORTHERN EDGE**, roughly a
  half-mile / a few blocks from the former-mall (arena) parcel. To the north are dense
  apartments; to the south (across the site) are Preston Hollow etc.
- **Verdict: this is "right behind it."** Maximum exposure to BOTH the upside (walkability to
  a $5B district + arena) AND the downside (construction, traffic, event nights). It is NOT
  the "1–2 mile appreciation sweet spot" — it's the adjacency zone, which cuts both ways.

## ADJACENCY ANALYSIS — upside vs. the costs most buyers underprice

### Upside
- **Walkable proximity to a $5B mixed-use district + (probable) NBA arena.** If it executes,
  a gated single-family pocket *adjacent* to a walkable district is scarce → scarcity premium.
- **District floor is real** (Premier under construction, Toyota/Panasonic equity, TIF
  backbone) — appreciation support even if the arena slips.
- **Gated + HOA = strong mitigant against event-night parking spillover** (the enclave can
  restrict/permit parking), which is the #1 thing that hurts homes next to arenas.

### The costs to underwrite (this position, specifically)
1. **You'd own through 5–10 YEARS of active construction.** Midtown is a ~10-yr build; the
   arena (if it proceeds) closes ~2028 and targets ~2031. That's **years of dust, truck
   traffic, road work on Preston/Alpha/Montfort, and noise** — right when you'd be living
   there. This is the most underpriced cost.
2. **Event-day traffic (if arena built):** 40+ NBA nights + concerts → congestion on
   Preston/Alpha ingress-egress. Gating protects parking, not the drive home.
3. **Timeline risk is yours to carry:** arena ~70–75% and ~2031; you pay today, payoff is
   years out and uncertain.

## FINANCIAL DILIGENCE
- **Is an "arena premium" already baked in?** The June–July arena news is public; a $599–669K
  ask on a 1992 3/2 may already lean on hype. Arena is ~70–75% and 5+ yrs out — **do NOT pay
  full arena premium now.** Anchor value to the *district* thesis + recent enclave comps.
- **HOA:** get dues, reserve study, special-assessment history, rules, and whether the HOA
  can be dissolved/assembled (gated HOA = hard to buy out → less teardown/assemblage upside,
  but more protection & scarcity).
- **Taxes/TIF:** the site is in a TIF district — confirm how (if at all) it affects the
  homeowner's Dallas County tax bill; model tax growth as the area appreciates.
- **1992 build capex:** foundation (N. Texas clay soil — get an engineer's foundation
  inspection), roof, HVAC age, plumbing. Budget for it.
- **Insurance:** get a quote; hail/wind in DFW matters.

## HOW IT MAPS TO THE PROBABILITIES (from prior analysis)
- District redevelopment ~**90%+** → supports buying near it.
- NBA arena on-site ~**70–75%** → optionality, not certainty — don't overpay for it.
- On/near 2031 timeline ~**50–60%** → you'll live through construction; payoff is long-dated.
- **Therefore:** this works as a **long-hold (7–10 yr) lifestyle + district-appreciation
  bet** — NOT a short-term flip. If the family/proximity-to-mom value is high and the horizon
  is long, the fundamentals support it *at the right price*. If the horizon is short or the
  price embeds a full arena premium, pass or negotiate hard.

## DILIGENCE CHECKLIST / QUESTIONS TO ASK
1. **Verify:** current list price, full price history, days-on-market (Redfin/agent).
2. **Orientation:** which way does 5750 face — does it **back onto Alpha Rd / the site**
   (construction + noise exposure) or **inward** (protected)? This alone can swing value.
3. **HOA docs:** dues, reserves, assessments, CC&Rs, parking/gate rules.
4. **Zoning:** pull the **City of Dallas PD (Planned Development)** for the district AND the
   enclave — is Encore protected low-density, or could it be upzoned/assembled later?
5. **City plans:** any Preston/Alpha widening, traffic mitigation, or arena-parking district?
6. **Inspections:** foundation (clay soil), roof, HVAC, drainage/flood (area had infra fights).
7. **Comps:** pull trailing 12–24 mo Encore $/sqft; compare to this ask; use the $485K/2,545sf
   listing and the 5632 April sale as anchors.
8. **Negotiation:** if there was a price cut and DOM is high, there's room — the arena is not
   a done deal and you should not pay as if it is.

## BOTTOM LINE
Good *strategic* location on a real, funded district transformation — but it's the **adjacency
zone, not the sweet spot**, so the construction-years drag and the unproven arena timeline are
real costs. **Buy it as a long-hold at a price justified by enclave comps + the ~90% district
thesis — not at a price that front-runs a ~70% arena that's 5+ years away.** Nail down which
way the lot faces and whether a price cut has already signaled seller flexibility.
