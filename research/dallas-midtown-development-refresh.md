# Dallas Midtown — Full Refresh (development-only, arena tracked separately)

Big refresh on the Beck Ventures "Dallas Midtown" redevelopment of the ~110-acre former
Valley View Mall site. Filtered to actual real-estate development; excludes the Mavs arena
(tracked in the arena docs) and civic/PID items (not decision-relevant).

## Headline: state is STABLE, not newly changed
No major net-new Midtown announcement since Phase 1 broke ground. **The Premier is still the
only vertical project actually under construction.** Everything above it remains master-plan
vision on a ~10-year horizon. The biggest new catalyst for the district is the **arena option
(June–July 2026)**, which is adjacent to — not part of — Beck's Midtown.

## PHASE 1 — "The Premier at Dallas Midtown" (the only real, funded, vertical project)
- **What:** 6-story mixed-use, **296 luxury apartments + ~13,500 sf ground-floor retail**,
  on a ~4-acre parcel at **Preston Rd & Dilbeck Ln** (Preston/LBJ).
- **Cost:** ~$85M.
- **Timeline:** ground broke **Nov 2025**; grading **April 2026**; **opens 2027**, first
  residents **first half of 2028**.
- **The team / capital stack (well-capitalized, not speculative):**
  - Developer/GC: **Anthem Development** / Anthem Commercial Construction
  - Equity: **Beck Ventures** + **PLT America** (Prime Life Technologies — a **Toyota +
    Panasonic** JV). Japanese corporate equity is a real vote of confidence.
  - Construction lender: **NexBank**
  - Architect: **Cross Architects**; civil engineering: **BGE Inc.**
- Read: this piece is real and financed. It happens regardless of the arena.

## THE MASTER PLAN (still vision — discount accordingly)
- **~$4–5B, ~67–110 acres, ~10-year rollout.** Envisioned: **~4,000 residential units**
  (apartments, condos, townhomes), **Class A office towers**, **hotel/hospitality**, a large
  **central park/green space**, plus retail/dining/entertainment.
- **No Phase 2 / second building / anchor office or hotel tenant has been announced yet.**
  The 2023 version was "275 condos / $80M"; it evolved into the 296-unit Premier — i.e., the
  plan has shifted with the market. Treat future-phase specifics as aspirational until a
  second groundbreaking.

## PUBLIC FINANCING / INFRASTRUCTURE (durability signal)
- The site sits in a **Tax Increment Financing (TIF) district** established **2015** for the
  Valley View–Galleria corridor (bundled with the old Southwest Center Mall area). The TIF
  **reimburses developers for streets/infrastructure** from the incremental tax value the
  redevelopment creates. This is real, committed public support for the district's backbone.

## HONEST SKEPTICAL CONTEXT (why timelines deserve a discount)
- The Becks bought Valley View **~13 years ago (~2012)**; "Dallas Midtown" has been
  "coming" since the **2015** TIF. It repeatedly stalled on **demolition issues and disputes
  with the city over who pays for infrastructure.** Mall demolition only finished **2023**.
- So the base rate here is **chronic delay.** The Premier finally breaking ground (2025–26)
  is the first hard proof of execution after a decade-plus of plans — genuinely a turning
  point, but the master-plan timeline should be read with heavy skepticism.

## NOISE FILTERED OUT (so it's not mistaken for Midtown news)
- **Knox Street / Knox & McKinney / 3333 Knox** — a *different* district (Knox-Henderson/
  Uptown, Trammell Crow). NOT Valley View Midtown. Ignore for this thesis.
- **Midtown Dallas Inc. HQ / "Resilience Midtown" homelessness center** — civic/PID
  governance, not development that moves home values.

## WHAT IT MEANS FOR THE HOMEBUYER THESIS
- **The floor is intact and improving:** a funded, under-construction, Toyota/Panasonic-backed
  first phase + a committed TIF backbone = the dead-mall-to-district transformation is real
  and progressing on its own, independent of the arena.
- **But it's early and slow:** only 1 phase is vertical; the $4–5B vision is a 10-year story
  with a 13-year history of delays. Don't underwrite near-term appreciation on the full
  master plan — underwrite it on the Premier + arena optionality + general corridor momentum.
- **Next real Midtown catalyst to watch:** a **Phase 2 groundbreaking** or a **named office/
  hotel anchor** — either would signal the district is compounding, not just starting.
