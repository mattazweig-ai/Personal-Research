# Deep Detail: Legends Global Hire + Cuban Lawsuit (as of July 23, 2026)

Full data behind the two July signals that confirm the Mavs arena deal is proceeding.

---

## PART 1 — WHO THEY HIRED: Legends Global

### The July 21, 2026 announcement (primary: Mavs press release)
- Mavs selected **Legends Global** in **two roles** for the "future venue project":
  1. **Owner's Representation** — an independent third party representing the Mavs
     **"from pre-design through closeout,"** protecting the team on **budgeting,
     construction schedules, quality, and risk control**, and acting as liaison to
     contractors and architects.
  2. **Premium Sales Partner** — Legends' sales team delivers **premium seating** for the
     new venue, with **dedicated staff on the ground.**
- **Quote — Mavs President Ethan Casson:** *"As we set out to redefine the premium fan
  experience in Dallas and beyond, we're excited to partner with Legends Global to help
  bring that vision to life."*
- Sources: nba.com/mavs (Jul 21), CelebrityAccess (Jul 21), D Magazine (Jul), The Real
  Deal (Jul 22).

### Who Legends is (company profile)
- **Founded 2008** as a stadium-hospitality JV of **Yankee Global Enterprises (NY Yankees)
  + Jerry Jones (Dallas Cowboys)** (originally with a Goldman Sachs / CIC Partners third).
- **Jan 2021:** **Sixth Street** (global investment firm, >$50B AUM) took a majority
  investment, valuing Legends at **~$1.3B.**
- **Aug 2024:** completed acquisition of **ASM Global** — the world's largest venue-
  management company. The combined entity operates under the **"Legends Global"** brand.
- Scale: **450+ venues** worldwide; **~165M guests/year.** Runs F&B/ticketing at the
  Cowboys' **AT&T Stadium** and **Yankee Stadium.**
- CEO: **Shervin Mirhashemi.**
- **Existing Mavs relationship since 2014** (in-venue merchandise) — this is an expansion,
  not a cold start.

### Their owner's-rep / construction track record (the relevant part)
- Owner's-rep arm built by acquiring **Nations Group** (athletic capital-facility owner's
  rep) and combining it with Legends' project-development team.
- Project team says it has delivered **>$12B of projects**, including:
  - **SoFi Stadium** (LA Rams/Chargers) — Legends was **project manager 2016–2020** and
    commercialized the 300-acre Hollywood Park district (naming rights, premium, sponsorship).
  - **BMO Stadium, Snapdragon Stadium, Lower.com Field, CPKC Stadium,** Univ. of Kansas
    **David Booth Memorial Stadium,** **Rogers Centre** reno, **Caesars Superdome** reno.
  - **New Highmark Stadium** (Buffalo Bills) — opening Fall 2026.
- Also engaged on **new venue projects for the Athletics (A's), Cleveland Browns, and
  Buffalo Bills** — plus "strong NBA experience."

### ⚠️ Blemishes worth knowing (context, not deal-killers)
- **Dropped from the Clippers' Intuit Dome:** the Clippers ended Legends' project-management
  role (April 2022); **CAA Icon** took over to keep it on time/budget.
- **$3.5M penalty + forced compliance officer** over pre-merger (ASM Global) conduct
  (antitrust/gun-jumping compliance matter).
- A CBS8 (San Diego) investigation raised **questions about the firm** tied to San Diego's
  new sports arena selection.
- Takeaway: heavyweight, credible owner's rep, but not a spotless record — normal for the
  space.

### Why this matters for you
Hiring an owner's rep **"pre-design through closeout"** AND a premium-seat sales team is the
behavior of a team that is **building**, not one about to walk from an option. This is the
clearest signal to date that they stayed in past the June 30 termination date.

## PART 2 — THE CUBAN LAWSUIT (filed ~July 8–9, 2026)

### What it is
- A **Texas Rule 202 petition** = pre-suit discovery (**depositions + document production**)
  — a legal precursor that lets Cuban investigate before filing a full lawsuit.
- Target: **Arena Development Intermediate, LLC (ADI)** — the same Dumont/Mavs entity that
  signed the Seritage option. (This is the buyer in the $50.76M deal.)

### What Cuban is demanding
- Records on the **financing** of the new arena.
- The **site exploration/identification** process.
- The **arena purchase contract, corporate structure, and related development
  opportunities.**

### Cuban's stake and claims
- Sold **majority control in Dec 2023 at a ~$3.5B valuation**; **retains ~27%** of the team.
- Claims his entities are **"contractually entitled to participate" as equity investors** in
  any future stadium/arena venture.
- Alleges a **Dec 2023 "handshake deal":** Cuban keeps authority over **basketball ops**;
  Dumont runs the **business side — real estate development + pursuit of an integrated
  destination resort casino in Dallas.** Cuban claims **Dumont "did not fulfill his end,"**
  freezing him out ("adversarial business practices").

### Read-through for you
1. **The deal is real and high-stakes** — real enough for a 27% owner to litigate over.
2. **Financing is genuinely opaque** — even Cuban (27%) is suing to see it, so it is not in
   any public document. This remains the biggest known-unknown.
3. **The casino/resort angle** is the strategic backdrop: the Adelson/Dumont family (Las
   Vegas Sands) has long eyed a Texas destination-resort casino; the arena + Dallas Midtown
   district fits that ambition if Texas ever legalizes casino gaming.
4. **Governance friction** is a modest execution risk, but it does not signal the deal is
   dying — the opposite, since they're simultaneously hiring builders.
- Sources: Front Office Sports, The Real Deal (Jul 9), NBC DFW, Yahoo/AP, Roundtable.
