## D. Mavs–Stars litigation (NEW — major)
- **Apr 2026:** Mavs dropped their tortious-interference (damages) claim after an Apr 2
  ruling in their favor; May jury trial cancelled.
- **~May 20–22:** Dallas Business Court (Judge Whitehill, ~94-pg opinion) **awarded the
  Mavs full control of the AAC**, finding the **Stars breached** by moving HQ to Frisco,
  triggering a buyout right over the Stars' AAC interest.
- **Stars appealed** to the Texas 15th Court of Appeals (ex-Chief Justice Wallace Jefferson
  on the team). **Not finally resolved** — appeal pending.

## E. Stars → Plano "The Bend" (NEW — major, parallel play)
- **June 2:** Stars signed a **formal (non-binding) LOI** with Plano for Willow Bend.
- **Structure (public-subsidy model):** ~107-acre arena site inside a **900+-acre TIRZ**;
  **Plano contributes up to $700M** of a ~$1B project; **city OWNS the arena/site** and
  **leases to Dallas Stars Enterprises for 30 years.**
- Developers: **Levin Holdings, Cawley Partners, Centennial Real Estate.** ~20,000 seats;
  Willow Bend demolition ~fall 2026; build 2028–29; open 2031–32. **"The Bend" proceeds
  with or without the Stars.**
- **Plano City Council hearing: June 8, 2026** (not yet held as of this run).

## Investor read-through (what these updates change)

1. **We can now triangulate the land.** Seritage = **~$2.54M/acre**. Apply (loosely) to
   ~104 acres → **~$260M** order-of-magnitude total land value. Heavily caveated — the
   Seritage parcel (former Sears box, frontage) may price above or below Beck's interior
   acreage, and Beck's basis from ~2012–13 is far lower, so **Beck's embedded gain is the
   real story.** Still, this is the first hard datapoint to anchor any model.

2. **Commitment is real but optionality is preserved.** Escalating, non-refundable carry
   ($169K → $127K/mo → $275K/mo) signals the Mavs are serious, but the **June 30 off-ramp**
   and **entitlements-gated close** mean this is still cancellable. Position around the
   edges; don't assume a 2031 arena is a done deal.

3. **Downgrade the casino-optionality thesis for Valley View — hard.** On-record denial +
   Mavs (not Sands) buyer entity + primary rout + entrenched Patrick. The LVS "Texas call
   option" still exists in the Irving land bank, but it's **further out and lower-probability**
   than our baseline implied. LVS remains a *Macau/Singapore* story with a *thin, slower*
   Texas kicker. Re-rate accordingly.

4. **The cleaner public-subsidy value-capture play right now is PLANO, not Dallas.** Plano's
   model is *explicit and formalizing* (TIRZ + $700M city money + city-owned arena), and
   **"The Bend" proceeds regardless of the Stars.** Collin County adjacencies + a dying mall
   with a funded redevelopment is arguably more underwriteable today than the still-opaque
   Mavs financing. Watch the **June 8** hearing as the first hard go/no-go.

5. **The political fault line is the barbell.** Maxie Johnson's opposition + D Magazine's
   "is Redbird subsidizing Valley View?" framing is the risk most likely to alter the TIF
   economics. This is now the single most important thing to monitor on the Dallas side.
