## The most useful NEW context (beyond the June 1 8-K)

### 1. Seritage is a distressed, motivated seller in wind-down
- Operating under a **shareholder-approved "Plan of Sale" (2022):** sell all assets, distribute
  proceeds, dissolve. **Going-concern doubt** disclosed.
- FY2025: ~$230.7M asset-sale proceeds, repaid ~$190M debt → **term loan down to ~$50M**, which
  **matures end of July 2026.** Cash ~$59M (Q1 2026). Only ~10 assets left (~0.8M sf + ~154 ac).
- CEO **Adam Metz** (Andrea Olshan departed ~Apr 2025); actively exploring **refinancing + a
  possible "strategic transaction" (i.e., sale of the whole company).**
- Say-on-pay **rejected** at the June 9 annual meeting; trustees got **<2/3** support (dissident
  shareholders). A company under pressure to monetize.
- **Read-through:** the counterparty had every incentive to lock in a headline price. Which frames
  how to read the $50.76M (below).

### 2. Price context — strong for Seritage vs. tax basis; book value NOT disclosed
- **Dallas County tax appraisal on the land ≈ $19.7M** (explicitly NOT commercial/GAAP value).
  So **$50.76M is ~2.6× the county appraisal** — a good price for a motivated seller.
- **GAAP carrying/book value of the Dallas parcel: NOT FOUND** in any reachable source. Cannot
  confirm whether $50.76M is above/below book. (Likely accounted for as an **unconsolidated
  equity-method JV** since co-owned with Beck/Life Time — unconfirmed.)
- Company-wide impairments exist (e.g., ~$18.8M for 9mo 2025); **no Dallas-specific impairment found.**
- No official Seritage NAV. Lead analyst **Aaron Chow (Elephant Analytics/Seeking Alpha):** calls
  the price **"good"** but the **closing timeline long**; SRG value band ~$2.25–$2.75/sh now, ~$4 by
  end-2027. (Aggregator book value/sh ~$5.88 — accounting, not NAV.)

### 3. The land's history — repeatedly tried, repeatedly stalled
- Marketed since **2022 via JLL as "Vista Commons"** (~17 ac), pitched as **"fully entitled,
  high-density master plan."**
- A prior **~2M sf mixed-use project with KDC Development** (groundbreaking ceremony 2019) was
  **abandoned by 2022.** A later ~400-unit luxury high-rise plan also dropped.
- Late 2024: the three owners (Beck, Seritage, Life Time) **"banded together to sell."**
- **No prior binding PSA that fell through** was documented — but the site has a long history of
  false starts. Relevant to execution risk.
- ⚠️ **Acreage discrepancy unresolved:** "Vista Commons" was ~17 ac; the Mavs option covers "20 ac."

### 4. Extra deal terms confirmed from the filing (beyond what we had)
- **Entitlements Period Expiration = earlier of Jan 28, 2028 or when buyer obtains entitlements.**
- Up to **~$4.5M total non-refundable option payments** over the option life.
- **Cross-conditioned** with the Beck/Life Time "Related PSA" — if that fails, this can fail.
- Filing states **"there is no assurance the buyer will ultimately exercise the purchase option."**
- **The full option PSA is to be filed as an exhibit to the next 10-Q (Q2, ~mid-Aug 2026)** — that
  document will contain the actual contract text AND the post–June-30 status.

## What this means for the homebuyer thesis
- The sophisticated buyer (Mavs) structured a **cheap, non-refundable OPTION** (~$169k down, ~$4.5M
  max carry) on a **motivated seller's** land — i.e., they bought **flexibility, not commitment.**
  Even they aren't "all-in" yet. That's the strongest argument against paying an "arena premium"
  for a nearby house today.
- The land's **decade of stalled plans** (KDC, high-rise, Vista Commons) is a caution: big visions
  here have repeatedly failed to materialize. The arena is the best anchor yet, but history says
  discount the timeline.
- **Watch the Q2 10-Q (~mid-Aug):** the PSA exhibit + subsequent-events note is the next hard,
  primary-source confirmation of whether the deal is proceeding.
