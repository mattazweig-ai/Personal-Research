# Master Report — Dallas Mavericks Arena @ Valley View / "Dallas Midtown" & the Surrounding Area

**Prepared:** 2026-07-01 · **For:** homebuyer/investor evaluating a purchase near the site
**Location:** former Valley View Mall, Preston Rd & I-635 (LBJ), Far North Dallas (~11 mi N of downtown)
**Sourcing note:** Compiled from multi-agent web research + SEC filings. Record portals (SEC
EDGAR, Dallas County Clerk, DCAD) and many news domains were HTTP-403 to our tools, so most
figures come from search snippets + previously verified filings. Confidence is flagged
throughout; treat pre-construction figures as directional and verify by exact address before buying.

---

## 1. Executive summary

- On **June 1, 2026** the Mavericks (via LLC **Arena Development Intermediate, LLC / "ADI"**)
  signed **option agreements** to buy **~104 of ~110 acres** at the dead Valley View Mall for a
  new arena + entertainment district. It is an **option, not a purchase** — nothing has closed.
- Terms are partly public **only because one seller (Seritage) is a public company** and filed
  an 8-K: **$50.76M for its ~20-acre slice (~$2.54M/acre)**; escalating non-refundable option
  carry; **first termination off-ramp June 30, 2026**; **closing not until Jan 31, 2028** (or 90
  days after entitlements). Beck Ventures' majority parcel + Life Time's piece: **terms undisclosed.**
- **As of July 1 we cannot confirm** whether the Mavs proceeded past the June 30 off-ramp — no
  new filing/news/record. Reasonable inference: they proceeded (default = carry payments begin),
  but that is **not confirmed**. Next hard confirmation ≈ Seritage **Q2 10-Q (~August)**.
- The arena is a **2031 target**, pre-entitlement: **no zoning approval, no disclosed financing
  mix, no cost, no renderings.** It can still die in due diligence.
- **The area is redeveloping regardless of the arena:** Netflix House opened (Dec 2025), the
  Galleria is being repositioned, and *Premier at Dallas Midtown* (296 apartments) is under
  construction (opens ~2027). The $4–5B "Dallas Midtown/International District" master plan
  beyond that is largely **conceptual**.
- **Market backdrop is soft:** Dallas values ~**−4.6% YoY** citywide; condo/Addison segments
  near the site down **~11–15%**; single-family 75248/75230 up mid-single digits. Buyer-friendly.
- **No data yet shows arena-driven home appreciation** — bullish "values will soar" claims are
  broker marketing, not transactions.

## 3. The players

- **Beck Ventures (Scott & Jeff Beck):** owns the majority of the ~110-ac assembly since ~2012;
  master-developer of "Dallas Midtown / International District." Low land basis → large embedded gain.
- **Seritage Growth Properties (NYSE: SRG):** ~20 ac (Sears-bankruptcy legacy); the reason any
  terms are public.
- **Life Time Inc.:** holds a portion; terms undisclosed.
- **Adelson/Dumont family:** own the Mavs (~$3.5B, 2023) and control **Las Vegas Sands**;
  Patrick Dumont is LVS chairman/CEO (since Mar 2026). **Casino explicitly ruled out here**
  (Welts on record); Mavs chose Valley View over Sands' Irving land — arena ≠ casino strategy.
- **Rick Welts:** Mavs CEO running the arena; built the privately financed Chase Center →
  signals a private-financing lean (unconfirmed for this project).
- **PLT America (Toyota + Panasonic JV) + Anthem Development:** equity/dev partners on Phase 1.
- **City of Dallas:** zoning, the TIF districts, the planned 20-ac park. Supportive leadership
  (Mayor Johnson) but **CM Maxie Johnson opposes** (south-Dallas equity concern).

## 4. The area / neighborhood (for a homebuyer)

**Character gradient over a short distance** — this is NOT one neighborhood:
- **The Midtown core (75240/75254):** historically a pure retail/office node, **renter-dominated**
  (75240 ~76% renters), lower median income (~$52–67k), now converting to new-build multifamily.
- **South = Preston Hollow (75230):** established, affluent, owner-occupied; median HH income
  ~$110k, homes ~$870k+ (up to estates). Safer.
- **West = Addison:** dense, walkable, **most restaurants per capita in the US**; Addison Circle
  TOD + new Addison Junction at the Silver Line station.
- **North/east = Far North Dallas / Prestonwood:** mix of aging apartments, condos, mid-priced SF.

**Schools (VERIFY BY EXACT ADDRESS — district split is real and material):**
- **Dallas ISD** covers the site: zoned HS is **W.T. White (TEA ~"B"/83, mid-tier)**.
- **Richardson ISD** covers pockets east/NE: **Prestonwood Elementary 9/10 (GreatSchools)** — strong.
- **Private-school corridor** just south: **St. Mark's, Hockaday** (both Niche #1 in TX), Greenhill, etc.

**Livability:**
- **Safety** improves as you move south: Far North Dallas ~42nd percentile; **Preston Hollow ~55th**
  (index estimates, not official DPD).
- **Transit:** DART **Silver Line opened Oct 2025** with **Addison Station** operational to the
  west; **BUT the Preston Rd & Coit stations nearest the site were cancelled** — no rail *at* the
  site (bus only), which is why a people-mover is proposed. 
- **Amenities:** Galleria Dallas + **Netflix House** (opened Dec 2025, ~100k sf) adjacent; Addison dining.
- **Flood zone: UNKNOWN — must verify** at msc.fema.gov by parcel before buying.

## 7. Traffic & access (see deals/mavs-arena-traffic-access-memo.md for the full memo)

- **No funded road expansion at the site.** I-635 was rebuilt in 2015 (LBJ Express); the active
  LBJ East project is east of US-75 and doesn't reach here; Montfort was *narrowed* 6→4.
- An NBA event ≈ **~6,700 vehicles**, needing **~5,000–7,000 (structured) parking spaces**.
- The binding constraint is **post-event egress** (garage exits → ramps → signals), not daily
  traffic. The site's **three limited-access routes (I-635, DNT, Preston)** give good dispersal —
  comps (United Center, The Battery) show freeway-fed suburban arenas clear fine *with active
  traffic management*. **Verdict: manageable, not a kill-shot — but mitigation is unfunded** and
  becomes a future public-cost ask. For livability, **~1–2 mi out beats walking-distance.**

## 8. Value-capture thesis & investor angles

- The money is in **land + development rights**, captured by **Beck** (low basis) and **ADI/Mavs**,
  with the city contributing via zoning + TIF. Outside investors can't touch the 104 acres.
- Comp: **The Battery Atlanta** — on-site parcels $5M (2014) → $644M (2025), ~$4.5B halo — shows
  the *magnitude* of value an anchor can create for parcel owners; halo reaches adjacencies.
- **Accessible angles:** halo-adjacent residential (~1–2 mi), the under-priced **Redbird/southern
  Dallas TIF-barbell** beneficiary, and **LVS** as a *thin/slow* Texas-optionality proxy (downgraded
  — casino ruled out here; Sands lost the March primaries; Patrick entrenched).

## 10. Bottom line for a homebuyer (not financial advice)

1. **Underwrite the house on its own merits** — schools (verify the district by address!),
   condition, commute, resale, flood zone — **as if the arena never comes.** Treat arena upside
   as a *free option you don't pay extra for.*
2. **Don't pay an "arena premium."** It's an option on an unfunded, un-zoned, 2031 arena that can
   still die. Sellers/agents citing June 1 are pricing a *maybe*.
3. **The market favors you right now** — buyer-friendly, soft condo/Addison segments, rent
   concessions. Time is on your side; no need to rush at peak uncertainty.
4. **Location within the area matters as much as the arena:** for schools/safety/value, the
   established quality is **south (Preston Hollow / RISD pockets)**, not the 75240 core; for arena
   upside without game-night pain, favor **~1–2 mi out** over walking-distance.
5. **Re-check the real signals before closing:** Seritage Q2 10-Q (~Aug), a Dallas zoning filing,
   and any financing disclosure. Those — not marketing — tell you the arena is truly live.
