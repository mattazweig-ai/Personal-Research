# Option Agreement — Public-Record Trail (research log)

**Question:** Find the Mavs–Beck option/offer for the ~104 acres — is it filed or leaked?
**Date run:** 2026-06-02 (two parallel research agents: press + public records)

## Verdict
- **Terms are NOT publicly disclosed and NOT leaked.** Confirmed it's a signed *option*
  (plural agreements) on ~104 acres; price, option fee, expiration, contingencies, and
  buyer/seller legal-entity names are all undisclosed. Bisnow: "terms weren't released."
- **No recorded instrument has been cited by any reporter** (no Memorandum of Option,
  no deed, no DCAD ownership change).
- **Caveat — inconclusive on records:** both agents were HTTP-403 blocked from the actual
  record portals (Dallas County Clerk, DCAD, OpenCorporates, TX SOS) in this environment.
  So "not found" reflects (a) press says undisclosed AND (b) we couldn't query the county
  index. A recorded memorandum could exist and simply hasn't been retrieved. Needs a
  manual search from an unblocked browser.

## Don't conflate (traps)
- The **"$85M"** figure circulating = the *Premier at Dallas Midtown* building, NOT the
  arena option price.
- **Las Vegas Sands is not named as a party** to this option in any Valley-View coverage.
  The Adelson/Dumont tie is to Mavs ownership generally. Treat "Sands signed this" as
  unverified until an entity surfaces.

## Leads worth chasing (the useful part)
1. **Sands' LLC signature.** Prior TX land buys used special-purpose LLCs at the Las Vegas
   Sands HQ address. The Irving ~108 acres (SH-114 & Loop 12) went through
   **"Village Walk RE 2 LLC"**, TX taxpayer no. **32090694657**, mailing **5420 S.
   Durango Dr., Las Vegas, NV**. → Search DCAD/SOS for new 2025–26 LLCs at that address or
   similar "* RE * LLC" pattern to ID the Valley View buyer entity (and learn whether it's
   a Mavs or a Sands vehicle — a real tell on strategy co-mingling).
2. **Underlying ownership to sign off:** Beck Ventures reportedly in a **JV with Life Time
   Inc.**; **Seritage Growth Properties** holds a portion (Sears-bankruptcy legacy).
   Confirm via DCAD parcel ownership.

## Manual search playbook (run from an unblocked browser)
- **Dallas County Clerk official records:** https://dallas.tx.publicsearch.us/ — grantor/
  grantee searches: "Beck Ventures", "Valley View", "Village Walk", "Mavericks", "Midtown";
  date 2025–2026; look for *Memorandum of Option / Memorandum of Contract*.
- **DCAD owner/parcel:** https://www.dallascad.org/SearchOwner.aspx — owner "Beck",
  address Preston Rd / Alpha Rd at LBJ; check 2025–26 ownership/deed changes; grab parcel
  account numbers + current owner entity.
- **TX SOS SOSDirect / Comptroller Taxable Entity Search** + https://opencorporates.com —
  new 2025–26 LLCs tied to Beck/Mavs/Sands; watch for the 5420 S. Durango Dr. address.
- **City of Dallas** — City Plan Commission / Council / TIF (Dallas Midtown & Valley View
  TIRZ) agendas referencing a development agreement or the arena site.

## Realistic expectation
Option price is almost always confidential this early. Best likely record yield = the
*existence* of a recorded memorandum + the **buyer LLC name** + parcel/acreage — not a
price. A full term sheet leaking would be unusual pre-closing.
