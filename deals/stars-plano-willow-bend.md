# Stars Arena @ "The Bend" / Shops at Willow Bend (Plano)

**Site:** **~76-acre** arena site (Park Blvd & Dallas North Tollway) at the dying Shops at
Willow Bend, Plano (Collin County) — *council-packet figure; earlier coverage said ~75/~107,
the ~107 appears to have conflated the arena site with the broader TIRZ.*
**Status (2026-06-08):** Formal **non-binding LOI** (signed by Stars/**Dallas Sports &
Entertainment LP**, CEO Brad Alberts) **on the Plano Council agenda for a vote TODAY, June 8**
— outcome not yet reported.
**Target:** Demolition ~fall 2026 · build 2028–29 · ~20,000-seat arena open 2031–32.

> **UPDATE 2026-06-08:** June 8 agenda also includes a **separate $15M incentive to Centennial**
> (mall owner): **$10M for demolition** (inline mall, theater, Dillard's, Crayola Experience) +
> **$5M for a visitor center** — first hard public money committed to the redevelopment
> *independent of* the arena. Reinforces "The Bend proceeds with or without the Stars."
> Meanwhile the **Mavs' Valley View item is reported as headed to Dallas City Council this week.**

---

## Why this is the *cleaner* value-capture play right now

The Mavs/Valley View deal is privately led and opaque on financing. The Stars/Plano deal
is the opposite — a **public-subsidy model that is being formalized in the open**, which
makes it more underwriteable today:

- **~$1B project; Plano contributes up to ~$700M**, funded from a **900+-acre TIRZ** and
  other city funds.
- **City of Plano OWNS the arena and site** and **leases to Dallas Stars Enterprises (DSE)
  for 30 years.** (Classic public-ownership / private-operator structure — the *opposite*
  of the Mavs' likely private-build model.)
- **Master developers: Levin Holdings, Cawley Partners, Centennial Real Estate** — branded
  **"The Bend."**
- **Key fact: "The Bend" proceeds with or without the Stars.** So the mall-redevelopment
  thesis exists independent of the arena — the arena is upside, not the whole bet.

## Investor angles
1. **Collin County adjacencies.** Plano/Far-North suburb is among DFW's strongest growth +
   income demographics. A funded TIRZ + named institutional developers + an anchor =
   textbook halo setup (cf. The Battery; see dfw-market/tif-and-value-capture.md).
2. **Public ownership lowers execution risk** vs. a private arena that needs cheap capital.
   The financing question that clouds the Mavs deal is largely answered here.
3. **The redevelopment happens regardless** — so even an arena no-go still drives a mall→
   mixed-use conversion in a prime Plano location.

## Risks / what's unresolved
- **LOI is non-binding;** June 8 council hearing is the first real go/no-go.
- **Mavs–Stars litigation not final:** Mavs won AAC control at trial (~May 20–22, Judge
  Whitehill, ~94-pg opinion; Stars breached by moving HQ to Frisco). **Stars are appealing**
  (15th Court of Appeals; ex-Chief Justice Wallace Jefferson). The buyout/control outcome
  could still shift timing and economics of the Stars' exit.
- **Public-subsidy politics:** $700M of city money invites the same scrutiny stadium
  subsidies always draw (cf. the academic Battery debate — value creation ≠ taxpayer win).
- **Both teams leaving the AAC by 2031** → downtown/Victory Park loses both anchors (Goldman
  Sachs campus survives as the new downtown anchor).
