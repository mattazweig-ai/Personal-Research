# IPO Oversubscription Comps — thread index

A comp set built to answer one question: **does the leaked "X-times-oversubscribed" book
actually predict how an IPO trades afterward** — and what does that say about **SpaceX
(pricing 2026-06-11)?**

## Files
- **`oversubscription-comps.md`** — the analysis. 36 US IPOs (Jan 2024→Jun 2026) + Saudi
  Aramco benchmark + SpaceX. Master table, oversubscription detail (sources + conflicts), the
  predictive test, and SpaceX situated.

## TL;DR
- **Oversubscription multiples aren't disclosed** — they're roadshow leaks (Reuters/Bloomberg/
  FT/CNBC). Patchy, anonymous, often conflicting. Only ~18 of 36 deals have any sourced number;
  the rest are `n/a` (never estimated).
- **The leaked multiple predicts the day-1 pop, not aftermarket quality.** In 2025 the pop was
  the *warning sign*: **7 of 8 of the most-hyped ≥20x books are now flat-to-deeply underwater**
  (Figma ~40x → +250% day-1 → now −36%; Gemini >20x → −85%; Klarna, Netskope, StubHub all
  down 49–59%). The quiet / below-range deals (CoreWeave, Viking, UL Solutions, Karman) were
  the best performers. Classic winner's-curse reversal.
- **SpaceX is only ~2x oversubscribed** — modest vs the 2025 hot books — but at a **fixed
  $135 price**, so the multiple isn't comparable. The real risk is the **~2.2x valuation
  step-up over the Dec-2025 $800B tender**, not the book.

## Sourcing discipline
Every oversubscription figure carries outlet + date + any conflict. Single-source / snippet-
only multiples are flagged. SpaceX live terms and the Figma 40x were independently re-verified.
