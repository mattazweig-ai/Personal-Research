# DFW Real Estate — Key Players

Durable reference. Who controls the land, the capital, and the rules in the
deals we track. Updated as we learn more.

## Developers / Landowners

- **Beck Ventures (Scott Beck)** — Owns/master-develops the ~110-acre "Dallas Midtown"
  assembly (former Valley View Mall). Bought ~2012–13; announced ~$4B vision. Spent a
  decade stalled until the arena anchor arrived. Partners on the first vertical building
  with **Anthem Development** and **PLT America**. Patient/long-hold landowner profile —
  the kind of basis that makes anchor-driven uplift hugely accretive.
- **PLT America (Prime Life Technologies America)** — JV of **Toyota Motor** + **Panasonic
  Holdings**. Equity partner on *The Premier at Dallas Midtown* (first vertical building,
  groundbreak Nov 2025; ~296 units + ~13.5k sf retail; ~2027 open). Signals deep-pocketed,
  patient corporate capital validating the site pre-arena.
- **Anthem Development** — Co-developer partner on Dallas Midtown vertical phase.
- **Seritage Growth Properties (NYSE: SRG)** — Owns ~20 of the 104 acres (Sears-bankruptcy
  legacy). Because it's *public*, its 8-K disclosed the arena option terms for its slice
  ($50.76M / ~20 ac; buyer = ADI). The reason any deal terms are public at all.
- **Life Time Inc.** — Holds a portion of the 104-acre assembly (third seller alongside
  Beck + Seritage). Terms undisclosed.
- **Arena Development Intermediate, LLC (ADI)** — The **Mavs' buyer vehicle** on the option
  (per Seritage 8-K). Notably NOT a Sands "Village Walk RE 2"-style LLC → signals the arena
  and the Sands casino land are separate strategies.

## Plano / Stars side (added 2026-06-03)

- **Levin Holdings, Cawley Partners, Centennial Real Estate** — Master developers of
  **"The Bend"** at Shops at Willow Bend, Plano (the Stars' proposed ~107-ac arena district).
- **City of Plano** — Public-subsidy partner: would own the arena and contribute up to
  ~$700M via a 900+-acre TIRZ, leasing to the Stars 30 yrs.
- **Dallas Stars Enterprises (DSE)** — The Stars' entity; proposed 30-yr arena lessee.
- **Judge Bill Whitehill (Dallas Business Court)** — Awarded the Mavs full AAC control
  (~May 2026); found the Stars breached by moving HQ to Frisco. Stars appealing (15th COA;
  ex-Chief Justice **Wallace B. Jefferson** on the appellate team).

## Team / Sports-Capital Owners

- **Adelson / Dumont family** — Bought the **Dallas Mavericks** (~$3.5B, NBA-approved
  Dec 2023). Principals: **Miriam Adelson** and **Patrick & Sivan Dumont**.
- **Patrick Dumont** — Mavs governor; became **Las Vegas Sands chairman/CEO (Mar 1, 2026)**.
  The connective tissue between the Mavs, the arena play, LVS, and the Irving casino land.
- **Las Vegas Sands (NYSE: LVS)** — Family's public gaming/real-estate vehicle. Holds
  **~1,000 acres near old Texas Stadium, Irving** as a casino-resort land bank (rezoned
  Mar 2025 only after the casino use was removed amid resident backlash). The liquid proxy
  for the family's Texas optionality (see deal doc §7 for caveats).
- **Rick Welts** — Mavs CEO leading the arena search; hired by Dumont Jan 2025. Built the
  Warriors' **privately financed Chase Center**. His presence is the strongest tell that
  the Mavs intend a private, PSL/premium-funded model.
- **Mark Cuban** — Sold majority but retains a stake and Basketball Ops; publicly endorsed
  the 635/Tollway access of the Valley View site.

## Public Bodies / Officials

- **City of Dallas** — Controls zoning, the TIF districts, and owns the planned 20-acre
  "Midtown Commons" park. Leadership supportive.
- **Mayor Eric Johnson** — Backed the arena search; favorable to the project.
- **Cara Mendelsohn (Council, Far North Dallas)** — Local council member; supportive.
- **Linda Koop & Tennell Atkins (former Council)** — Architects of the **TIF "barbell"**
  linking Dallas Midtown/Valley View to Redbird/southern Dallas.
- **NCTCOG** — Regional planning org; potential funder of a Silverline "people mover."
- **Dan Patrick (Lt. Gov.) / TX Senate** — The wall blocking casino legalization (killed
  2025; next shot 2027).

## Other DFW threads

- **Dallas Stars** — Splitting from the Mavs (legal dispute over the AAC). In talks with
  **Plano** for a ~$1B arena-district at **Shops at Willow Bend** (~75 acres). Also floated:
  Frisco, The Colony, Arlington, Fort Worth.
- **City of Plano / Collin County** — Suitor for the Stars; a parallel mall-to-arena
  redevelopment in fast-growing Collin County.
- **Goldman Sachs** — ~$709M, 5,000+ job campus under construction at Victory Park
  (downtown), anchoring downtown even as the teams eye exits.
- **Netflix House @ Galleria Dallas** — Experiential anchor ~2 mi from Valley View;
  reinforces the Far North Dallas entertainment cluster.
