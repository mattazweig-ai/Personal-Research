# Personal Research — DFW Real Estate

A working knowledge base for real estate investing, with a primary focus on the
**Dallas–Fort Worth (DFW)** market. This repo is where we build durable expertise:
submarkets, major developments, key players, zoning/TIF mechanics, deal structures,
and how anchor projects (arenas, corporate campuses, casinos) move land values nearby.

The goal is not to summarize the news. It's to **think like investors** — surface
angles, name who captures the upside, stress-test the consensus, and keep a dated
watchlist of catalysts.

## How this is organized

```
README.md                         ← you are here (index + operating principles)
deals/                            ← deep dives on specific situations / sites
  dallas-midtown-mavs-arena.md    ← FLAGSHIP: Mavs arena @ Valley View / Dallas Midtown
dfw-market/                       ← durable reference that outlives any one deal
  key-players.md                  ← developers, owners, public bodies, the personalities
  tif-and-value-capture.md        ← how TIF / development rights move land value (DFW lens)
watchlist.md                      ← dated catalysts to track across all threads
```

## Operating principles (how I work as your partner)

1. **Follow the land and the rights, not the headline.** The team/arena is the
   marketing. The money is in who controls the land and the development rights around it.
2. **Always name the value-capture path.** For every thread: who pays, who profits,
   and is there an instrument *we* can actually touch?
3. **Push back on consensus.** Where the local narrative is lazy (e.g. "no rail = it
   fails"), say so and show the comp.
4. **Separate confirmed from rumored.** Flag what's an option vs. a closed deal, an
   LOI vs. a signed term sheet, legal vs. illegal.
5. **Keep a dated watchlist.** Theses live or die on catalysts with dates.
